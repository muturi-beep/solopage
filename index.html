Case Study · Agency Website

Omnipulse Agency

A live, client-facing agency website. The goal was a clear, confident online presence that communicates what the agency does and turns visitors into enquiries.

Overview

Omnipulse Agency is a deployed website built to represent a brand online. It presents the agency's offering, establishes credibility, and gives visitors an obvious next step.

The Challenge

Agency websites live or die on clarity. Visitors need to understand what's being offered within seconds — on any device — and find a way to make contact without hunting for it.

The Approach

Structure first: a clear hierarchy of sections, a strong above-the-fold statement, and a consistent visual language throughout. Everything is built to be responsive by default rather than patched for mobile afterwards.

Key Features

  • Responsive layout across mobile, tablet and desktop
  • Clear service presentation with a logical content hierarchy
  • Direct enquiry / contact route
  • Performance-conscious build with lightweight assets

Case Study · Voting Music App

Baze Music

A voting-driven music platform where listeners discover, rank and vote on tracks — giving emerging artists a fair shot at the audience's attention.

Overview

Baze Music is a live web application that turns music discovery into a community activity. Instead of algorithmic feeds deciding what rises, listeners vote on tracks and the leaderboard reflects the audience's real taste.

The Challenge

Emerging artists struggle to be heard above algorithmic noise, while listeners are stuck with recommendation feeds they don't control. Baze needed a fair, transparent system where every vote counts and talent can rise on merit.

The Approach

Key Functionality

  • Vote on tracks to influence the ranking in real time
  • Live leaderboard driven by audience votes
  • Artist and track profile pages
  • Responsive, mobile-first interface for voting on the go
